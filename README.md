## Five mistakes that void your own cashback

Most declined cashback isn't the platform's fault. It's one of these:

1. **Not clicking through from the portal.** The tracking cookie only exists if the journey starts there. Browse the deal on your own and buy directly, and the commission chain never forms.
2. **Ad blockers and privacy extensions.** They strip the tracking cookie along with the ads.
3. **Unofficial coupon codes.** A code pasted from a random site can override the portal's referral attribution and decline the whole transaction, even if the code itself works. Use codes shown inside the portal or from official sources [S10][S17].
4. **Running two portal extensions at once.** Rakuten's and TopCashback's browser extensions can overwrite each other's tracking. Disable the one you're not using for that purchase [S9].
5. **Switching portals mid-journey.** Click through one portal, then detour through another before checkout, and the last cookie wins — or neither does.

One expectation to set: referral bonuses sound more generous than they are. A $40 bonus for referring a friend goes to the *referrer*, and it only pays out once the new member has earned $10 in payable cashback within 180 days — as a new member you get whatever one-off new-user promotion is running at the time, not that $40 [S17]. Don't sign up expecting a cash bonus to land in your account on day one.
